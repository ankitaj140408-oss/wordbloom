How WordBloom helps you remember

Each word moves through a simple spaced-revision cycle:

1. Learn
Read the meaning and example. Write your own sentence.
2. Recall
Try to remember the meaning before revealing it.
3. Apply
Say or write a fresh sentence using the word today.
4. Repeat
Revision intervals grow as you remember: 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
